replication of inulin findings from paper:

Baxter NTSchmidt AW, Venkataraman AKim KS, Waldron C, Schmidt TM2019.Dynamics of Human Gut Microbiota and Short-Chain Fatty Acids in Response to Dietary Interventions with Three Fermentable Fibers. mBio10:10.1128/mbio.02566-18.https://doi.org/10.1128/mbio.02566-18
using v4 region of the 16s rRNA collected from the human gut in fecal samples before and during a inulin dietary intervention in order to stablish relationships between the fermentable fiber and short chain fatty acid (SCFAs) production
Raw sequencing reads and metadata, including SCFA concentrations, are available through the NCBI Short Read Archive under accession number SRP128128.https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra/?term=SRP128128

In order to answer the question: 
Does inulin treatment increase SCFA production (i.e., acetate, butyrate, propionate), and is this associated with a shift in fiber-fermenting bacteria?

- *Null Hypothesis:* Microbial diversity and scfa production does not vary during inulin treatment.
- *Alternative Hypothesis:* An inulin treatment will stimulate scfa production and shift microbial composition.
